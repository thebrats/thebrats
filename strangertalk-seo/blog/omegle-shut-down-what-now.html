Omegle Shut Down – What Now?

By Amit Kumar · 10 October 2026

For 14 years, Omegle was the place to "talk to strangers". In November 2023 it shut down for good. Millions of users – including a huge number from India – suddenly had nowhere to go. So what happened, and where can you chat now?

Why did Omegle close?

Omegle's founder, Leif K-Brooks, said running the site had become impossible – financially and psychologically. The main reasons were the cost of fighting misuse and the many legal cases about users being harmed, especially minors. The lesson is simple: a random chat site has to take safety seriously from day one.

What should a good Omegle alternative have?

  • Strict 18+ rule – no children on the platform.
  • Report and ban system – so bad users can be removed quickly.
  • No signup, no personal data – your privacy stays protected.
  • A real contact person – in India, sites must have a Grievance Officer under the IT Rules, 2021.
  • Works on phone – most Indians chat on mobile.
