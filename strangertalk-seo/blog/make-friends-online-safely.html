How to Make Friends Online Safely in India

By Amit Kumar · 10 October 2026

New city, college holidays, or just bored at night? Talking to new people online can be fun and can even turn into real friendships. Here's how to do it the right way.

1. Start with an easy opener

"Hi" works, but a question works better. Try:

  • "Hey! Kya chal raha hai aaj?"
  • "What's one show you'd recommend right now?"
  • "Team chai or team coffee?"

2. Talk about interests, not personal details

Music, movies, cricket, games, food and travel are great topics. Your city is fine to mention. Your address, school, college, office, phone number and full name are not.

3. Be respectful

The person on the other side is real. No abuse, no pushing for photos, no rude comments. If the chat isn't working, just say bye and move on.

4. Know the red flags 🚩

  • They ask for money, UPI, OTP or gift cards.
  • They quickly ask for your number, Instagram or photos.
  • They want to move to video and ask you to undress – this is often sextortion.
  • They send links and ask you to click.
  • Their story sounds too good (job offer, investment, "lottery").

See a red flag? Skip, report, and if you've been threatened, call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

5. Take it slow

If you really click with someone, keep talking on the platform for a while before sharing any contact. Never meet a stranger alone; if you do decide to meet, choose a busy public place and tell a friend.
