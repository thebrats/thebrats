Frequently Asked Questions

Is StrangerTalk free?

Yes. StrangerTalk is free to use with unlimited random chats.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start chatting without signing up. We do not ask for your name or phone number.

Who can use StrangerTalk?

Only adults aged 18 or older. Users under 18 are not allowed and are blocked if found.

Can I chat in Hindi?

Yes. Most users are from India and chat in Hindi, Hinglish or English. See Hindi stranger chat.

Is StrangerTalk safe?

StrangerTalk uses automatic filters, a report button and bans to keep chats safe. You should still never share personal details or money with strangers. Read our safety tips.

How do I report someone?

Use the Report button in the chat, or email our Grievance Officer at strangertalk.in@gmail.com. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours.

Are my chats saved?

No. Chat content is not stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is StrangerTalk an Omegle alternative?

Yes. Omegle closed in November 2023. StrangerTalk offers random anonymous chat focused on users in India. Learn more.
