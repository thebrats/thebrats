Abhi Chat Shuru Karein

StrangerTalk kyun?

  • Bilkul free – koi charge nahi.
  • Signup nahi – na naam, na phone number. Site kholo aur chat karo.
  • Desi log – India bhar ke log, apni bhasha mein baat.
  • Phone pe chalta hai – koi app download karne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Safe – gaali aur galat behaviour pe filter, report button aur ban.

Kaise chat karein? (3 steps)

  1. strangertalk.in kholo aur confirm karo ki aap 18+ ho.
  2. Start dabao – hum aapko ek random ajnabi se jod denge.
  3. "Hi" bolo! Baat jam nahi rahi? Next dabao aur naye insaan se milo.

Baat shuru karne ke liye kuch ideas

  • "Hi! Aap kahan se ho?" (city batana theek hai, address nahi)
  • "Aajkal kaunsi web series dekh rahe ho?"
  • "Cricket follow karte ho?"
  • "Ek gaana suggest karo jo aajkal repeat pe hai."
