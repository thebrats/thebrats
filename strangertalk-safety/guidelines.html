Community Guidelines

Last updated: 10 October 2026

StrangerTalk is a place to meet new people and have fun conversations. Ye rules sabki safety ke liye hain. Inhe todne pe warning, temporary ban ya permanent ban ho sakta hai.

✅ Do

  • Be respectful and friendly — izzat se baat karein.
  • Skip the chat if you are not comfortable.
  • Report anyone who breaks these rules.
  • Keep your personal details private.

🚫 Don't — zero tolerance (instant permanent ban)

  • Anything involving minors – any user under 18 or any sexual content involving minors. We report this to law enforcement (cybercrime.gov.in).
  • Nudity or sexual acts on camera, or sharing sexual images/links.
  • Threats, blackmail or sextortion.
  • Recording or sharing someone's video/chat without consent.

🚫 Don't — leads to ban

  • Gaali, abuse, bullying or harassment.
  • Hate speech against religion, caste, gender, region or sexuality.
  • Spam, advertising, links, promotions or selling anything.
  • Asking for money, UPI, OTP or personal information.
  • Pretending to be someone else.
  • Violence, self-harm, drugs or weapons content.

How enforcement works

  1. Automated filters block banned words, phone numbers and links.
  2. Reports are reviewed. Multiple reports may lead to an automatic temporary ban.
  3. Serious violations result in a permanent ban by IP/device.

Think you were banned by mistake? Write to us via the Grievance page.

Need help right now?

  • Cyber crime: 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in
  • Women helpline: 181 · Police emergency: 112
  • Mental health support (Tele-MANAS): 14416
