Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Amit Kumar ("StrangerTalk", "we") processes your personal data when you use strangertalk.in, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the IT Act, 2000.

Short version: You can chat without creating an account. We collect the minimum data needed to run the service and keep it safe. We do not sell your data.

2. Legal basis

We process data on the basis of your consent (which you give when you use the service and accept the consent notice) and for legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, including complying with law and responding to legal requests.

3. Children

StrangerTalk is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly process data of children. If you believe a child is using the service, report it at our Grievance page and we will act immediately.

4. Sharing

We share data only with: (a) service providers who help us run the site (hosting, analytics, payments) under confidentiality; (b) government or law-enforcement agencies when legally required; (c) to protect users' safety. We never sell personal data.

5. Your rights (DPDP Act)

  • Get information about the data we hold about you
  • Ask for correction or erasure of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (stop using the service and clear your browser data)
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity

To use these rights, email strangertalk.in@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law.

6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption and reasonable security practices. No system is 100% secure; please never share sensitive information with strangers.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date above.
