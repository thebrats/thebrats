Online Stranger Chat – Safety Tips

Last updated: [DATE]

Ajnabi logon se baat karna mazedaar hai, par apni safety pehle. In tips ko follow karein:

1. Apni pehchaan chhupa kar rakhein

  • Don't share your full name, phone number, address, school, college or office.
  • Don't share your Instagram, Snapchat or WhatsApp with someone you just met.
  • On video, make sure nothing in your background reveals where you live.

2. Sextortion se bachein

  • Never undress or do anything on camera you wouldn't want recorded — the other person can record the screen.
  • If someone threatens to leak your video and asks for money: don't pay, stop replying, save evidence and report at 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.

3. Scam se bachein

  • Never send money, UPI, OTP or gift cards to anyone you meet here.
  • Don't click links strangers send you.
  • Be careful of "investment", "job" or "lottery" offers.

4. Uncomfortable lage to

  • Press Skip / Next immediately — you don't owe anyone a conversation.
  • Press Report so we can ban them.
  • Don't agree to meet strangers in person.

Helplines

  • Cyber crime: 1930 · cybercrime.gov.in
  • Police: 112 · Women helpline: 181
  • Tele-MANAS mental health: 14416
